2 years in the dark

 

AT LAST! AT LAST! AT LAST!

I slew The Minotaur with a rewwing- no- roaring fire. It all happened so suddenly, I can scarcely believe my luck!

I'll tell you all about it. The night (or as was it day?) prior, I had rooked up this beastish hop of a bale-fire and allowed it to tumble uncontrulled. Hot smoke choked up the corridors, and I was a drunken tumbler, rumracking myself through these maze lanes, pulling, pushing, conducting it, I faithfully believed. Between my singing pink eyelids, I spot the tail of The Minotaur sleeping in some passage I had never (and will never) explore, and not a moment after that, the hall seemed to turn into itself, and everything, everywhere, all at once, like the seizures of a dying sun, my fire, my fire overwhelmed her passage!

Popping to my ears were the busting madhouse noise of ancient stone blocks fire spalling. It smelt fouling awful. I make a fast thrust with my spear, and then I fall over.

As I hobble over a dream into my afterfact wreckage, sooty ash taste in my mouth, I knew that what had happened was real. But I was lying in the dark all the same.

The Minotaur's screech bounces into this room, my room now, where I had fallen. I am not proud of it, but I felt guilty and begged her for mercy. I rattled and shuddering, curled myself into a ball for a wunking raping revenge-all. But her revenge never came. My attack on The Minotaur was successful. We would never meet again, I think she was dying. I felt sad and I missed her. Oddly, I felt sad and I missed her.

 

I laid there in the dark confused for a while. I am lost in a maze. I am very lost and very deep underground. I try to do the moral calculus on whether or not this is my eternal punishment from The Gods.

The Minotaur, the beautiful and magnetic intelligence of these tunnels, was cleared at last. You will laugh, but there were times I dreamed about us getting married, having a happy wedding, somewhere here in this dark labyrinth I built.

Looking up, awake, but dreaming into the dark; an efferent supernatural whim pulls me away from the stones under my back, into this or that direction, wandering pilgrim in foreign chapels. Until I recount Theseus and the solution to his own puzzlefucked journey, the thread he spooled; the one thing that unfucks you from the labyrinth!

I brought no such spool of thread, and the labyrinth makes no sense because I built it myself. I am constantly rebuilding it without choice or willingness... but when I recalled The Solution to Theseus's Puzzlefucking Predicament, my left hand (I remember clearly it was my left hand, my non-dominant hand) shot up and clenched tight, as if to catch the thought. I didn't dare loosen my grip. The thread. The thread. The thread. The thread. The thread. The Thread. The thread.

It was a start (or an end), and between my index finger and thumb I carefully, delicately, beggingly pinch the air where I believed the tail of the thought was in physical space.

 

I make one hell-fearing, back-crawling, wrist-aching scootch backwards; ever so slightly pulling myself down (or up) the thread; a spider-mime playing one creepy charade in the dark; my shape is not human; A drip of air into my book lungs and I flutter my pedipalps and I am CHARLOTTEIFIED; I'm the most beautiful, intelligent and vi$hcious

spider that ever was; You will love my silk; You will love me, You must, you fucking cricket.
